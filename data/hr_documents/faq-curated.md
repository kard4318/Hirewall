# People Team FAQ

Short answers to the questions the People Team receives most often. The full rules are in the related policies.

## 1. Frequently asked questions

### 1.1 Where can I see my remaining leave days?

Your leave balance, including carried-over days, is shown in the People Portal under "My time off". The People Portal is the only place where personal leave and pay records are shown; the knowledge assistant cannot see them.

### 1.2 Who do I contact with an HR question?

Contact the People Team at people@northwind.example.test. The team answers within two working days. For urgent questions, call the People Team line during core hours.

### 1.3 When is payday?

Salaries are paid on the 28th of each month. If the 28th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the last working day before it.

### 1.4 How do I update my bank details or address?

Update your bank details and home address in the People Portal under "My profile". Changes made before the 20th of the month apply to that month's payroll.

### 1.5 Can I speak to someone confidentially?

Yes. You can ask the People Team for a confidential conversation at any time by writing to people@northwind.example.test with "Confidential" in the subject. Only the People Team member you speak to will see the details, unless there is a risk to someone's safety.

### 1.6 How do I report a change in my working hours?

Talk to your manager first. If you agree on a change, your manager submits it in the People Portal and the People Team updates your contract within two weeks.

## 2. About this FAQ

The People Team updates this FAQ every quarter. If a question you have is not covered here or in the policies, write to the People Team and they will help you directly. Suggestions for new FAQ entries are always welcome, especially from new joiners who see our processes with fresh eyes.
