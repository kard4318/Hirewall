# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Northwind Labs works in a hybrid model. This policy explains how often you can work remotely, how to work from abroad, and what support you receive for your home office.

## 1. Hybrid working

### 1.1 Remote days

You may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The other two days are spent in the office; most teams use Tuesday and Thursday as their shared office days. Agree your remote days with your manager and record them in the team calendar.

### 1.2 Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can organise your time flexibly, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

### 1.3 Remote work from another city

Working from another city in the same country counts as a normal remote day. You do not need extra approval, but your usual weekly limit of 3 remote days still applies.

## 2. Working abroad

### 2.1 Allowance

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Days abroad count towards your weekly remote days.

### 2.2 Approval

Working abroad needs written approval from your manager and the People Team. Submit the request in the People Portal at least 30 days before your first day abroad, including the country and the dates. Requests submitted later may be declined.

### 2.3 Restrictions

Some countries are excluded for tax, data-protection or security reasons. The People Team checks every request against the current list and will tell you if a destination is not allowed. Customer data must never be accessed from public networks without the company VPN.

## 3. Home office support

### 3.1 Home office stipend

Every employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 400 EUR to buy a desk, a chair or other equipment for working at home. Claim the stipend through the expenses process within your first 3 months, with receipts. The stipend is paid once per employee, not every year.

### 3.2 Equipment from the office

You may take a monitor and a keyboard home if your team agrees. Register borrowed equipment with IT so that it can be tracked and returned when you leave.

## 4. Changing the arrangement

Your manager may ask you to come to the office more often for onboarding, workshops or project milestones, with at least one week of notice. If you need a permanent change to your hybrid arrangement, for example for health or caring reasons, talk to your manager and the People Team.
