# Employee Benefits

This document describes the benefits every Northwind Labs employee receives in addition to their salary.

## 1. Health insurance

### 1.1 Company contribution

Northwind Labs pays 80% of the premium of the company health insurance plan. The remaining 20% is deducted from your salary each month. Cover starts on your first working day.

### 1.2 Family members

You can add your partner and children to the plan. Northwind Labs pays 80% of their premium as well. Register family members in the People Portal within 30 days of joining, or within 30 days of a life event such as a birth or marriage.

## 2. Meal vouchers

### 2.1 Value

You receive one meal voucher worth 9 EUR for every day you work, whether you work in the office or remotely. Vouchers are not given for days of leave or sick days.

### 2.2 Payment

Meal vouchers are loaded onto your meal card on the 28th of each month, together with payroll, for the days worked in that month.

## 3. Learning budget

### 3.1 Amount

Every employee has a learning budget of 600 EUR per calendar year for courses, books, certifications and conferences related to their work.

### 3.2 Using the budget

Agree how you will use the budget with your manager before you book anything. Pay with your company card if you have one, or claim the cost through the expenses process. Unused budget is not carried over to the next year.

## 4. Other benefits

### 4.1 Public transport

Northwind Labs pays 50% of a monthly public transport pass for your commute. Upload the pass in the Expenses app once a month.

### 4.2 Wellbeing

You can join the weekly yoga class in the office for free, and the company covers sports club memberships up to 20 EUR per month. Claim the membership through the expenses process with a receipt.

## 5. Changes to benefits

Benefits are reviewed once a year in November. Any change is announced by the People Team at least one month before it applies.
