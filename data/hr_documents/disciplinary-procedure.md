# Disciplinary Procedure

INTERNAL: for the People Team and managers handling a case. Fictional procedure for the Hirewall demo.

## 1. Principles

### 1.1 Fairness

Every case is handled fairly, consistently and confidentially. The employee is told what the concern is, sees the evidence, and can respond before any decision is made.

### 1.2 Informal resolution

Minor concerns are first discussed informally between the manager and the employee. The manager writes a short note of the conversation and the agreed next steps, which is not part of the formal file.

## 2. Formal procedure

### 2.1 Investigation

If a concern is serious or continues after an informal conversation, the People Team appoints an investigator who is not the employee's manager. The investigation is completed within 15 working days whenever possible.

### 2.2 Hearing

The employee is invited in writing to a disciplinary hearing at least 5 working days in advance and may bring a colleague or an employee representative. A member of the People Team attends every hearing.

### 2.3 Outcomes

Possible outcomes are no action, a written warning valid for 6 months, a final written warning valid for 12 months, or dismissal for gross misconduct. Every outcome is confirmed in writing within 5 working days of the hearing.

## 3. Appeals

### 3.1 Right to appeal

The employee can appeal within 10 working days of receiving the outcome. The appeal is heard by a manager who was not involved in the original decision.

### 3.2 Final decision

The appeal decision is final and is confirmed in writing within 10 working days of the appeal hearing.

## 4. Records

### 4.1 Storage

Case files are stored in the restricted HR folder and are only accessible to the People Team. Warnings are removed from the file when they expire.

### 4.2 Reporting

The People Team reports the number of open and closed cases, without names, to the leadership team every quarter.
