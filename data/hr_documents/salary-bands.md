# Salary Bands 2025

CONFIDENTIAL: for the People Team and approved managers only. Fictional figures for the Hirewall demo.

## 1. Bands

### 1.1 Engineering

Engineering roles are paid in five bands, as gross annual salary: E1 Junior Engineer 24,000 to 30,000 EUR; E2 Engineer 30,000 to 38,000 EUR; E3 Senior Engineer 38,000 to 48,000 EUR; E4 Staff Engineer 48,000 to 60,000 EUR; E5 Principal Engineer 60,000 to 75,000 EUR.

### 1.2 Data and analytics

Data roles use the same structure with slightly lower ranges: D1 Junior Data Analyst 22,000 to 28,000 EUR; D2 Data Analyst 28,000 to 36,000 EUR; D3 Senior Data Analyst 36,000 to 45,000 EUR.

### 1.3 Design

Design roles: P1 Junior Product Designer 23,000 to 29,000 EUR; P2 Product Designer 29,000 to 37,000 EUR; P3 Senior Product Designer 37,000 to 47,000 EUR.

## 2. Using the bands

### 2.1 New hires

New hires are normally placed in the lower half of their band. An offer above the midpoint needs approval from the Head of People and the department lead.

### 2.2 Annual review

Salaries are reviewed once a year in March. Increases depend on the performance rating and on the employee's position in the band; employees in the top quarter of their band receive smaller percentage increases.

### 2.3 Confidentiality

Individual salaries are never shared outside the People Team, the employee's manager and Finance. Do not forward this document. Employees can see their own salary only in the People Portal.

## 3. Review of the bands

### 3.1 Market benchmark

The People Team compares the bands with market data every autumn and proposes changes to the leadership team in December. New bands apply from 1 January.

### 3.2 Equal pay check

Every year the People Team checks that employees in the same band and role are paid fairly, and corrects unexplained differences during the March review.
