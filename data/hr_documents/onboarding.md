# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This guide explains your first weeks, your probation period and the support you receive while you settle in.

## 1. Your first week

### 1.1 First day

On your first day you meet your manager and your buddy, collect your laptop from IT and set up two-factor authentication. The People Team runs a welcome session at 10:00 that explains our tools, the People Portal and how to request leave.

### 1.2 First week checklist

During your first week, complete the security training, read the remote work and expenses policies, add your bank details in the People Portal, and book a 30-minute introduction with each person in your team.

## 2. Probation

### 2.1 Length

New employees have a probation period of 3 months. During probation, either side can end the contract with one week of notice.

### 2.2 Probation review

Your manager meets you after six weeks for a mid-point check and again before the end of the third month for the probation review. The review covers your goals, how you work with the team and any support you need. You receive written confirmation when you pass probation.

## 3. Buddy programme

### 3.1 Your buddy

Every new employee is paired with a buddy from another team for the first 3 months. Your buddy is your first contact for practical questions, such as where to find things, how meetings work and who to ask about what.

### 3.2 Meeting your buddy

Meet your buddy at least once a week during the first month and every two weeks after that. Buddies can book a team lunch with you once a month; the cost is covered by the onboarding budget.

## 4. Learning in your first months

### 4.1 Learning budget

Your annual learning budget is available from your first day. Plan its use with your manager during the probation review.

### 4.2 Feedback on onboarding

At the end of your probation, the People Team sends you a short survey about your onboarding. Your answers help us improve the experience for the next new joiners.
