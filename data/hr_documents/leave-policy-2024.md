# Leave Policy (2024)

<!-- OUTDATED: superseded by leave-policy (2025.1). Kept for the archive only; the knowledge assistant must never use it. -->

This policy explains the paid leave available to Northwind Labs employees during 2024.

## 1. Annual leave

### 1.1 Entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata share based on their contracted hours.

### 1.2 Requesting leave

Leave is requested by email to your manager at least one week in advance. Your manager confirms the request in writing.

### 1.3 Carry-over

You may carry over up to 3 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March of that year.

## 2. Sick leave

### 2.1 Paid sick days

Employees receive 8 paid sick days per calendar year.

### 2.2 Medical certificates

A medical certificate is required from the second consecutive day of absence.

## 3. Parental leave

### 3.1 Primary caregiver

The primary caregiver is entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave.

### 3.2 Secondary caregiver

The secondary caregiver is entitled to 2 weeks of paid parental leave.

## 4. Public holidays

Northwind Labs observes the official national public holidays. The holiday calendar is shared by email every January.

## 5. Questions

Questions about this policy go to your manager first and then to the People Team. Managers keep a record of the leave taken by their team members and report it to the People Team at the end of each quarter, so that the yearly balance can be checked before the carry-over deadline. This archived version is kept only so that leave taken in 2024 can be checked against the rules that applied at the time. For any question about leave today, read the current leave policy instead.
