# IT Equipment and Security Policy

This policy covers the laptop and accessories you receive from Northwind Labs and the security rules for using them.

## 1. Laptops

### 1.1 Standard equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop, a charger, a headset and a laptop bag on their first day. Developers and designers can choose between two standard models during onboarding.

### 1.2 Refresh cycle

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. IT contacts you about two months before your laptop reaches three years of use to arrange the replacement. If your laptop is broken earlier, IT repairs or replaces it after a check.

### 1.3 Personal use

Limited personal use of your laptop is allowed, such as reading the news or private email during breaks. Do not install games, file-sharing tools or software that is not needed for work.

## 2. Security

### 2.1 Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for every company account, including email, the People Portal, the Expenses app and source code repositories. Use the company authenticator app; text messages are only allowed as a backup.

### 2.2 Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 24 hours to it-help@northwind.example.test, even if you think the device is locked. IT can then lock and wipe the device remotely. If the device was stolen, also report it to the police and send IT the report number.

### 2.3 Passwords

Use the company password manager for all work passwords. Never share passwords with colleagues, including IT: IT staff will never ask for your password.

## 3. Software

### 3.1 Installing software

Install software only from the Software Center on your laptop. If you need a tool that is not listed, request it through the IT help desk; IT checks the licence and security before approving it.

### 3.2 Updates

Security updates are installed automatically. Restart your laptop when you are asked to, at the latest within three days.

## 4. Leaving the company

Return your laptop, charger, headset and any borrowed equipment on your last working day. IT wipes all devices before they are reused.
