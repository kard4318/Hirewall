# Expenses and Reimbursement Policy

This policy explains which work expenses Northwind Labs reimburses, how to claim them, and when you are paid back.

## 1. What can be claimed

### 1.1 Eligible expenses

You can claim reasonable costs you pay personally while working for Northwind Labs: business travel, accommodation, meals during business trips, client meals approved in advance, training costs that are not covered by the learning budget, and the home office stipend.

### 1.2 Not reimbursed

Fines, personal entertainment, alcohol outside approved client events, upgrades to first class and costs for family members are not reimbursed.

## 2. Receipts

### 2.1 When a receipt is needed

A receipt is required for every expense above 25 EUR. Upload a clear photo or the original electronic receipt to the Expenses app. For expenses of 25 EUR or less, a short description is enough, but you must still record the date and purpose.

### 2.2 Lost receipts

If you lose a receipt for an expense above 25 EUR, fill in the lost-receipt form in the Expenses app and explain what happened. Repeated lost receipts can lead to claims being declined.

## 3. Submitting a claim

### 3.1 Deadline

Submit every claim within 30 days of the date of the expense. Claims submitted later are only accepted in exceptional cases with written approval from Finance.

### 3.2 Approval

Claims up to 500 EUR are checked by Finance against the receipts and do not need manager approval. Claims above 500 EUR need approval from your manager before Finance processes them. Your manager receives the request automatically in the Expenses app.

### 3.3 Currency

Claims in other currencies are converted to EUR at the exchange rate of the expense date. Card fees for foreign payments can be claimed as part of the same expense.

## 4. Reimbursement

### 4.1 Payment date

Approved expenses are paid together with your salary. Payroll runs on the 28th of each month; claims approved before the 20th are included in that month's payroll, and later approvals are paid the following month.

### 4.2 Questions

If a claim is declined or you think a payment is wrong, contact Finance through the Expenses app. Finance answers within five working days.
