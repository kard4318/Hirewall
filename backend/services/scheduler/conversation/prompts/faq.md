# Interview FAQ (fictional company)

- How long is the interview? About 45 minutes, including a few minutes for your own questions.
- What is the format? A video call with the hiring manager and one person from HR. The calendar invitation contains the video link.
- Who will I meet? The hiring manager for the role and an HR partner.
- What should I prepare? Re-read the job description, have one or two examples of past work ready to discuss, and test your camera and microphone beforehand.
- Do I need to bring documents? No documents are needed for the first interview.
- How do I reschedule or cancel? Reply to your confirmation email and the HR team will help you find a new time.
- Can I choose a time outside the offered options? The assistant only offers free times from the HR calendar. For special arrangements, ask HR.
- Is there a technical test? Not in the first interview. HR will explain any next steps afterwards.
- When will I hear back? HR contacts every candidate after the interview, whatever the outcome.
