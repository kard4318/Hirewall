# Leave Policy

This policy explains the paid leave available to every Northwind Labs employee. It replaces the 2024 leave policy from 1 January 2025.

## 1. Annual leave

### 1.1 Entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata share based on their contracted hours. Employees who join or leave during the year receive a share proportional to the months worked, rounded up to the nearest half day.

### 1.2 Requesting leave

Leave is requested in the People Portal. For absences longer than three working days, submit the request at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines within five working days. Requests are approved in the order they are received when several team members ask for the same period.

### 1.3 Carry-over

You may carry over up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March of that year; any carried-over days not used by then are lost. Unused leave beyond the 5 days lapses on 31 December and is not paid out, except for remaining days when you leave the company.

## 2. Sick leave

### 2.1 Paid sick days

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick days do not reduce your annual leave and cannot be carried over.

### 2.2 Reporting and medical certificates

Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence and record the sick day in the People Portal. A medical certificate is required when you are sick for more than 3 consecutive days, that is from the fourth consecutive day of absence. Upload the certificate to the People Portal within two working days of your return.

## 3. Parental leave

### 3.1 Primary caregiver

The primary caregiver of a newborn or newly adopted child is entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The leave can start up to two weeks before the expected birth or placement date.

### 3.2 Secondary caregiver

The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave, to be taken within six months of the birth or placement.

### 3.3 Notice

Tell the People Team about planned parental leave at least 8 weeks before it starts, so that cover can be arranged. Your role, salary and benefits are protected during parental leave.

## 4. Public holidays

Northwind Labs observes the official national public holidays. The People Team publishes the holiday calendar for the coming year every January. Public holidays that fall during your annual leave are not counted as leave days.
